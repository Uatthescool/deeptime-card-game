# Deck 6: The Great Migration (African Mammals) (on paper)

Living and fossil mammals of Africa. Every Creature must be found in Africa; it may be found elsewhere too, so its continent mark can show a second continent (marks follow the species on the card). It may borrow a few Creatures but should have its own identity. The Biome and the deck's shape aren't decided.

## Roster so far

| Creature | Level | Scientific name | Continents | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| African Bush Elephant | 3 | Loxodonta africana | Africa | The largest living land animal; the larger of the two African elephant species. |
| Giraffe | 2 (proposed) | Giraffa camelopardalis (Northern giraffe) | Africa | Since August 2025 the IUCN recognizes four giraffe species; *G. camelopardalis* is the first named and keeps the classic name. An Artiodactyl, so Tower of Giraffes can find it. Proposed Tags: Overreach, Retaliation. |
| Lion | 2 (probably) | Panthera leo | Africa, Asia | Tag: Pride. Wild lions also live in India's Gir Forest. |
| Plains Zebra | 1 (proposed) | Equus quagga | Africa | The most widespread of the three zebra species; the extinct quagga was one of its subspecies. Proposed Tags: Herd, Elusive. |
| Okapi | 1 (proposed) | Okapia johnstoni | Africa | The giraffe's only living relative; Congo rainforest. An Artiodactyl. Proposed Tags: Solitary, Elusive. |
| Blue Wildebeest | 1 (proposed) | Connochaetes taurinus | Africa | A hotter version of the Steppe Bison. The species of the Great Migration. Tags: Specialized, Herd (puts 2 random Herd Creatures into the hand). |
| Cape Buffalo | 2 | Syncerus caffer | Africa | The nominate subspecies of the African buffalo. An Artiodactyl. Tags: Circle Defense, Retaliation. Level 2 for the danger it poses to lions, not its size (about the Steppe Bison's). |
| Hippopotamus | 2 | Hippopotamus amphibius | Africa, Europe | Borrowed from Deck 4. Lived across Europe in the Pleistocene, including Britain about 125,000 years ago. |
| Phiomia | 1 | Phiomia serridens | Africa | Borrowed from Deck 4. |

All are Herbivores except the Lion. The living species are Quaternary and get the `living` flag.

## New Tag

- **Pride** (Lion), draft rules text: "This Creature also counts as Herd and Pack Hunter. While defending, it gets +50 Battle Power for each other friendly Herd Creature. While attacking, it gets +50 Battle Power for each other friendly Pack Hunter."

## Supports so far

- **The Great Migration:** a Continuous Event in the style of Ice Age. Effect undecided; it might favor Herd Creatures or Herbivores.
